Good to know

The web version of sanggup always carries the latest lessons. The Android test build v0.2.0 was bundled just before two additions (the tindak web link and the lost-wallet stage), so those two are web-only until the next APK. The app is designed to preserve your progress when you update in place; an automated check proving exactly that is still being verified, so treat it as a goal, not a guarantee, until it passes.
